# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

**What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**  
**Ans.:** Any physical, verbal, or visual action of a sexual nature done with the intent to humiliate and offend a person, or asking for sexual favors of any kind without their voluntary involvement, causes sexual harassment. 

**What would you do if you experience or witness any incident, or repeated incidents, of such behaviour?**  
**Ans.:** Certain steps can be taken based on the severity of the incident;
* I'll step inside and ask the offender to stop doing it.
* I'll encourage the victim to take a firm stand and be assertive in warning the offender.
* If it continues, I'll encourage the victim to file a formal complaint. I might take the initiative myself if needed.
* I'll stay vigilant about my surroundings and such offenders.
