# Focus Management
### 1. What is Deep Work
**Question 1:** What is deep work?  
**Ans.:** Deep work is the ability to focus on mentally demanding tasks for a sustained period of time without being distracted.

### 4. Summary of Deep Work Book
**Question 2:** According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?  
**Ans.:** Dedicate time blocks for hard and valuable tasks and cut out the distractions. Minimize low-value tasks and use that time for valuable tasks.

**Question 3:** How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?  
**Ans.:** I can pick a task and dedicate a definite time slot to that task and keep away all distractions for that period. And once I'm done with the time slot, I'll spend some time on a break or other low-value tasks.

### 5. Dangers of Social Media
**Question 4:** What are the dangers of social media, in brief?  
**Ans.:** Social media may cause addiction and non-deliberate usage patterns, resulting in wastage of time, attention, and the ability to focus. It may also cause psychological issues like loneliness, anxiety, etc.
