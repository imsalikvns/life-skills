# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit
**Question:** Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.  
**Ans.:** Grit is the ability to keep going despite lacking natural abilities like IQ or talent. A growth mindset is how you build grit.  

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset  
**Question:** Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.   
**Ans.:** The video talks about how fixed and growth mindsets affect the outcomes of learning efforts. Where the fixed mindset belives in abilities to be inherent and avoids effort, potentially leading to failure, the growth mindset views failure as an event and persists despite facing it.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control
**Question:** What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?  
**Ans:** Internal locus of control is the belief that I'm responsible for my fate, actions, and their outcomes. The key point in this video is that blaming failures on outside factors is giving them the driving force of our lives.  

## 4. How to build a Growth Mindset
**Question:** What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?  
**Abs.:** Key points mentioned by the speaker are:
* Believe in your ability to figure things out.
* Question your assumptions.
* Prepare your own roadmap for a long-term growth mindset.
* Honor your struggles. Accept downfalls.
