## 4. Sleep is your superpower
**Question:** Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation.  
**Ans.:** Here are a few points;  
* Sleep is essential for learning, memory, and overall brain function.
* Lack of sleep can negatively affect the heart, immune system, hormones, and mental performance.
* Consistently getting too little sleep is linked to higher risks of serious health problems.
* Sleep loss cannot be fully compensated for by sleeping longer later.
* Maintaining a regular sleep schedule and a comfortable, cool sleeping environment can improve sleep quality.

**Question:** What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?  
**Ans.:** 
* Maintain a consistent sleep and wake-up schedule every day.
* Keep your bedroom cool, dark, and quiet to create a sleep-friendly environment.

## 5. Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise
**Question:** Paraphrase the video - Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points.  
**Ans.:** Here are a few points that I know from the video and my own experience of exercising:
* Exercise improves mood by increasing brain chemicals such as dopamine.
* Regular exercise improves focus, attention, reaction time, and memory.
* Exercise strengthens the hippocampus, which plays an important role in forming long-term memories.
* Long-term physical activity strengthens brain functions involved in attention and decision-making.
* Regular exercise may help protect the brain against age-related cognitive diseases such as Alzheimer’s.
* Even simple aerobic activities such as brisk walking or climbing stairs can provide brain benefits.

**Question:** What are some steps you can take to exercise more?  
**Ans.:** 
* Set aside 30 minutes for exercise at least 3–4 times a week.
* Start with simple activities like walking, jogging, or cycling and gradually increase the intensity.
